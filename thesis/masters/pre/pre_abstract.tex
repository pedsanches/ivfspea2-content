\begin{abstract}{IVF/SPEA2: A Hybrid SPEA2 Algorithm with In Vitro Fertilization for Multi-Objective Optimization}
% \looseness=-1: o mesmo ajuste do resumo, para que abstract e keywords caibam numa página (2026-10-10).
\looseness=-1
This dissertation proposes IVF/SPEA2, which couples the in vitro fertilization (IVF) method, an intensification module, with the multi-objective evolutionary algorithm SPEA2, and evaluates whether it beats the host under the same number of objective-function evaluations. Assuming that SPEA2 density penalises local intensification, the coupling gives each secondary donor a primary donor distant from it in objective space, continues cycles while mean fitness improves, and limits the module's share of evaluations. On 51 ZDT, DTLZ, WFG and MaF instances, with 60 runs of 100,000 evaluations, inverted generational distance (IGD) as the primary metric, hypervolume (HV) as the secondary one and Holm correction, IVF/SPEA2 records against SPEA2 in IGD (wins/ties/losses) 22/3/3 with two objectives and 15/7/1 with three, and 19/3/2 and 12/3/0 on the 39 instances not used for tuning; on three engineering problems, 1/2/0, where the two ties are undetected differences, not equivalence. In IGD, the median probability that an IVF/SPEA2 run beats a SPEA2 run ($A_{12}$) is 0.756, and the median relative difference is 1.15\%; all four losses occur in WFG. In an ablation with the initial parameters, the donor and cycle rules did not beat the variant without them (50 ties, one loss). Against their own hosts (30 runs, Benjamini--Hochberg), the couplings to SPEA2, NSGA-III and NSGA-II win on 33, 22 and 1 of 51 instances in IGD, a descriptive ordering. Static descriptors showed limited predictive performance outside the training suites; early archive turnover reaches balanced accuracy 0.754, but its decisions coincide with the WFG-versus-rest split on 49 of 51 instances, and the controller based on it recorded more losses than wins against IVF/SPEA2 without control (2/40/9 in HV). Against SPEA2 variants, canonical methods and modern approaches, all at default settings, losses outnumber wins in 4 of 28 comparisons. The improvement belongs to the complete implementation on this suite, without isolating the role of the module, the rules, the configuration or the host.
\end{abstract}
