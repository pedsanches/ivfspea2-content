\begin{abstract}{IVF/SPEA2: A Hybrid SPEA2 Algorithm with In Vitro Fertilization for Multi-Objective Optimization}
This dissertation evaluates whether IVF/SPEA2 improves SPEA2 at a fixed budget, compares configurations, coupling variants and IVF couplings to other hosts, positions it against other comparators, and examines its activation. Motivated by the hypothesis that SPEA2 density may penalise intensification, the in vitro fertilization (IVF) coupling assigns a dissimilar primary donor to each secondary donor, continues cycles based on mean fitness, and uses a budget gate. Across 51 ZDT, DTLZ, WFG and MaF instances with 60 runs of 100,000 evaluations, IGD and HV as primary and secondary endpoints, and Holm correction, IVF/SPEA2 records against SPEA2 (wins/ties/losses) 22/3/3 for two objectives and 15/7/1 for three, and 19/3/2 and 12/3/0 on the 39 instances not used in tuning. Over the 51 instances, the median IGD $A_{12}$ is 0.756 and the median relative difference between IGD medians is 1.15\% in favour of IVF/SPEA2; all four losses occur in WFG. Against the host on three engineering problems: one win and two cases without detectable difference. Against the variant without the two decisions, an initial ablation records 50 non-detections and one loss; the historical tuning contrast changes formulation and parameters, and the main comparison does not isolate the module. In IGD, IVF beats its own host on 33 (SPEA2), 22 (NSGA-III) and one (NSGA-II) of 51 instances, a descriptive ordering of pipelines. Against seven context comparators, the win--loss balance is unfavourable in 4 of 28 contrasts. A static regression reanalysis with fold-contained response transformation and a joint DTLZ+MaF holdout yields cross-family Spearman correlation 0.150. Early archive turnover reaches balanced accuracy 0.754 (signal and window selected before the folds) but decides as the WFG-versus-rest partition on 49/51 instances, and the per-run controller using it did not beat always-on IVF/SPEA2 (2/40/9 in HV). The complete implementation improves its host on most instances of this suite; attribution to decisions, configuration or host is not established.
\end{abstract}
