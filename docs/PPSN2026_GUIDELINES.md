## Scope & Topics

PPSN is a biannual open forum on natural models, iterative optimization heuristics,
machine learning, and AI approaches. Welcomes work from rigorous mathematical results
to carefully crafted empirical studies.

### Paper Categories (based on PPSN 2024 proceedings)

1. Combinatorial Optimization
2. Genetic Programming
3. Fitness Landscape Modeling and Analysis
4. Benchmarking and Performance Measures
5. Automated Algorithm Selection and Configuration
6. Numerical Optimization
7. Bayesian- and Surrogate-Assisted Optimization
8. Theoretical Aspects of Nature-Inspired Optimization
9. Evolutionary Machine Learning and Neuroevolution
